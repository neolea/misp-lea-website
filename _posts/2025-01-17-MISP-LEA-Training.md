As part of our initiative to enhance collaboration and knowledge sharing with law enforcement agencies (LEAs), we are pleased to invite you to a free one-day training session hosted by CIRCL and limited to LEA members. This exclusive event is tailored specifically for LEAs and will focus on demonstrating and teaching the effective use of the [MISP-LEA](https://www.misp-lea.org/) infrastructure, including the AIL data lake.

Event Details

- **Date:** Tuesday, 4th February 2025
- **Time:** From 09:00 until 16:30
- **Location:** CIRCL - Computer Incident Response Center Luxembourg
  c/o "Luxembourg House of Cybersecurity" g.i.e.
  122, rue Adolphe Fischer
  L-1521 Luxembourg
  Grand-Duchy of Luxembourg

Content of the session:

- Hands-on training on MISP-LEA infrastructure ([MISP](https://www.misp-project.org/) and [AIL](https://www.ail-project.org/))
- In-depth exploration of the AIL data lake of MISP-LEA project
- Practical demonstrations and use-case scenarios

If you are working for a LEA, we encourage you to register for this training opportunity. 

Registration Link: [https://pretix.eu/circl/fkq78/](https://pretix.eu/circl/fkq78/) (lunch included)

For further details about MISP-LEA or any queries contact us directly at **info@misp-lea.org**.

We look forward to your participation.
