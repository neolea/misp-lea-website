As part of our initiative to enhance collaboration and knowledge sharing with law enforcement agencies (LEAs), we are pleased to invite you to a free half-day training online session given by CIRCL and limited to LEA members. This exclusive event is tailored specifically for LEAs and will focus on demonstrating and teaching the effective use of the [MISP-LEA](https://www.misp-lea.org/) infrastructure, including the AIL data lake.

Event Details

- **Date:** Thursday, 22nd May 2025
- **Time:** From 14:00 until 17:00
- **Location:** Online Session 

This session is foreseen for the LEAs who couldn't join the session of last February in Luxembourg. 

Content of the session:

- Hands-on training on MISP-LEA infrastructure ([MISP](https://www.misp-project.org/) and [AIL](https://www.ail-project.org/))
- In-depth exploration of the AIL data lake of MISP-LEA project
- Practical demonstrations and use-case scenarios

If you are working for a LEA, we encourage you to register for this training opportunity. 

Registration Link: [https://pretix.eu/circl/afrqr/](https://pretix.eu/circl/afrqr/)

For further details about MISP-LEA or any queries contact us directly at **info@misp-lea.org**.

We look forward to your participation.
